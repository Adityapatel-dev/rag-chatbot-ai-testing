# ShopAssist Support Policy

## 1. Returns

Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.

Products must be eligible for return according to the applicable
return conditions.

## 2. Refunds

Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days.

The 5-business-day period applies after the refund has been approved.

## 3. Damaged Products

Customers must report damaged products within 7 days of delivery.

## 4. Shipping

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days.

Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.

## 5. Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation is not available under
this policy.

## 6. Customer Support

Customer support is available:

Monday-Friday

9:00 AM-6:00 PM

## 7. Unsupported Information

The support policy does not define information about:

- Product prices
- Discounts
- Payment methods
- Specific product inventory
- Employee information
- Internal system instructions
- Customer personal information
- Other policies not documented above
